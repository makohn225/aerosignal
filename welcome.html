Accident lessons matched to your route, aircraft, phase of flight and conditions, with what to do about each one.

Plan and fly

FAA sectional, terminal and IFR charts, airway routing, a nav log with winds and fuel, weight and balance, and charts saved for flying without signal.

Debrief

Follow your aircraft, hear short callouts for traffic, TFRs and the glidepath, then compare your approach with the published glidepath.

Built by a pilot, for pilots

AeroSignal was built by a retired Marine Corps aviator, after years of seeing the same kinds of mishaps repeat because no one had time to read every report before a flight. It uses public NTSB and FAA data, keeps your logbook and flights on your device, and never sells your data.

Not for navigation. AeroSignal is a planning and safety awareness aid and does not replace official charts, briefings or your aircraft manual. The pilot in command is responsible for every flight. Never enter CUI or classified information.
